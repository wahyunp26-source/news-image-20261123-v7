# Font licences

The glyph atlases (`atlas_*.png` + `atlas_*.meta`) are rendered from the
Liberation fonts (Liberation Serif / Liberation Sans), which are licensed
under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org/). The atlases are generated at build time by
`gen_fonts.py` from the system-installed Liberation fonts.

The world-map landmask (`worldmap.txt`) is derived from Natural Earth 110m
land polygons (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/), which are in the public
domain.
